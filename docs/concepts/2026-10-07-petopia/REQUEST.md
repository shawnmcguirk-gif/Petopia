Synapse — Petopia

Design Petopia, the Pets & Wildlife centre within the Synapse family platform.

Petopia should be a comprehensive, warm, intelligent application for managing the animals we own or care for, while also helping the family discover, identify, understand and support wildlife around them.

This should NOT feel like a basic pet-management or veterinary app.

The vision is:

Petopia — Your world of pets & wildlife.

It should combine:

* Pet profiles
* Health and veterinary records
* Diet and nutrition
* Weight and development tracking
* Medication and treatment
* Care routines
* Reminders
* Documents
* Photos and memories
* Costs
* Insurance
* Animal knowledge
* Wildlife identification
* Wildlife sightings
* Garden wildlife
* AI-assisted advice and research
* Family participation
* Synapse integration

Design it so that virtually any animal can be represented.

Examples include dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters, guinea pigs, horses, reptiles, fish, aquarium inhabitants, chickens, parrots and other birds.

It should also support wildlife such as garden birds, foxes, hedgehogs, squirrels, frogs, butterflies, bees and other insects.

30. SAFETY & TRUST

Petopia must never pretend AI advice is equivalent to veterinary diagnosis.

Clearly distinguish:

* Stored veterinary records
* Information extracted from documents
* Owner observations
* General reference information
* AI suggestions
* Veterinary advice

For potentially urgent symptoms, encourage appropriate professional veterinary care rather than attempting diagnosis.

Wildlife advice should similarly avoid encouraging users to handle potentially dangerous, injured or protected wildlife inappropriately.

⸻

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Approach this as if we are building the best integrated family animal platform available, rather than reproducing an existing pet app.

The differentiator is that Petopia understands:

the animal + its health + its care + its diet + its history + its costs + its documents + its memories + its household + the wildlife around the household.

Think carefully about the underlying entities, relationships, provenance, timelines, permissions, ingestion architecture and Synapse integration.

Design for the simple case first:

Add Banoffee → photograph → basic profile → feeding → weight → vet records → reminders.

But ensure the architecture can ultimately support:

Dogs + cats + fish + birds + horses + reptiles + garden wildlife + habitats + sightings + species knowledge.

The experience should remain warm and approachable for a family while the underlying record keeping is structured, reliable and extensible.

Petopia should ultimately answer two questions extremely well:

How are the animals we care for doing?

and

What animals are sharing our world with us?
